01 / Philosophy

Taste · leverage · code

Not an agency with AI added on. Not a shop that sells prompts.

We are a systems studio. Product sense, design judgment, and engineering sit in the same room — and they direct the models. Speed is the byproduct. Quality is the point.

Static files rot. A Figma library that never ships is already archive. We keep the source of truth in GitHub: tokens, components, and the product itself. The system can keep building after the engagement ends because the decisions live in the repo, not in someone’s head.

Human taste at every layer

AI does not replace direction. It extends it. We decide what is good; the machines multiply the good.

Design lives as code

Tokens and components in git — the same source the product ships from. No second system to go stale.

A system that keeps deciding

You don’t leave with a deck. You leave with a language agents can extend without drifting off-brand.

04 / Engagement

Who it’s for · how it feels

Who it’s for

Founders who want a partner that ships, not a vendor that presents.

  • 01 Early teams building the first version that has to last — brand, product, and site as one system.
  • 02 Operators tired of Figma files that have drifted from production, and of agencies that vanish at handoff.
  • 03 Not for moodboards, pitch decks, or “just make it with AI.” If you want a picture of a product, we are the wrong studio.

Working together

A short, sharp engagement. Then the system belongs to you.

  • 01 We start in the repo. Yours, or one we open. Access on day one. No kickoff theater.
  • 02 Taste, then leverage. We set the language. Agents extend it under review. You see pull requests, not status slides.
  • 03 You keep the source. Tokens, components, and the product. We stay on if you want a studio in the loop — not a retainer for meetings.
